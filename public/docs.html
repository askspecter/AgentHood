01Introduction

AURN turns an idea into a live, tradable coin in under a minute — with the fees flowing back to the creator, not the platform.

Most launchpads assume you already have a wallet, some ETH, and the patience to bridge, connect, and configure. AURN removes all of that. You sign in with X, a wallet is minted for you, and you launch. Everything under the hood is real: a genuine ERC-20 on Robinhood Chain, a locked Uniswap V3 pool, on-chain fees you can claim.

02Getting started

  1. Open eska.fun and tap Sign in with X. That's your only account — no email, no password.
  2. Get your wallet. Signing in mints a wallet tied to your X account automatically. Nothing to install.
  3. Fund it with ETH. Your wallet needs a little ETH for gas. Copy your address from the Receive tab and send ETH to it on Robinhood Chain.
  4. Launch or trade. You're ready — create your first coin, or trade any coin on the chain.
Good to know

Gas is paid in ETH. Launches and trades are signed for you by your X wallet server-side, so there's no extension pop-up to approve.

03Your X wallet

When you sign in with X, AURN derives a wallet from your X account — deterministically, so the same account always maps to the same wallet. There is no seed phrase to back up and no key database to leak: the key is derived on demand from a master secret and your X user id.

  • No seed phrase, no extension. Your X login is the key.
  • Custodial by design. AURN can sign on your behalf so launching and trading are one tap. This is the trade-off that makes it frictionless.
  • You can export. Export your private key any time from Settings to move funds to a wallet you fully control.
Treat it like a hot wallet

Because it's custodial and lives online, keep only what you're actively launching or trading with. Move larger balances to a self-custodied wallet.

04Launch a coin

The create flow is a short wizard. Nothing is deployed until the final step.

  1. Name. Give your coin a name and ticker.
  2. Look. Pick a visual style — AI generates the coin's art (or upload your own).
  3. Soul (optional). Add a personality, tagline and lore that become the coin's description.
  4. Review & launch. Confirm, and your coin is deployed and live.

What happens on-chain

  • A real ERC-20 is deployed through the pons factory with a fixed supply of 1,000,000,000.
  • A Uniswap V3 pool (paired against WETH, 1% fee tier) is created and the liquidity is locked.
  • The coin is tradable immediately and appears in the feed.
Cost

Launching costs a small fixed fee (~0.0005 ETH) plus gas. You can optionally make a first buy in the same transaction.

05Trading

Every AURN coin trades in a Uniswap V3 WETH pool. Prices shown are live pool prices, and swaps are quoted before you sign so the number on screen is the number you get.

  • Buy / sell with one tap, signed by your X wallet.
  • Any coin. Paste any Robinhood Chain token address to trade it, not just AURN launches.
  • Slippage is adjustable per trade.
Safety first

Run the built-in honeypot check before buying an unknown coin — it simulates a buy and an immediate sell, so a token you can't sell reveals itself before you spend anything.

06Graduation

As a coin trades and its pool fills, it works toward graduation. Once the paired liquidity reaches the threshold (~4.2 WETH), the coin graduates and its market matures. Graduated coins are marked in the feed and keep trading on their Uniswap V3 pool.

07Creator fees

Every trade on your coin generates pool fees — and most of them are yours.

60% Creator 30% Protocol (pons) 10% AURN

Fees accrue in two assets — the coin itself and WETH — and can be claimed anytime from your coin's page, signed by your X wallet. You never unlock the liquidity to collect them.

  • 60% → you, the creator. Forever, claimable whenever there's volume.
  • 30% → the protocol (pons), fixed on-chain.
  • 10% → AURN, which funds $AURN buyback & burn and operations.

08The $AURN token

$AURN is the platform's own token, designed to be deflationary: the AURN share of fees is used to buy back and burn $AURN. More launches and more volume mean more burned.

09Architecture

Robinhood Chain

AURN runs on Robinhood Chain — an Arbitrum Orbit L2 (chain ID 4663), EVM-compatible, with ETH for gas. Fast, cheap, and permissionless to deploy on.

pons

Coins are deployed through pons, the launch factory and fee locker. Each launch has a fixed 1B supply and locked liquidity, and the locker splits pool fees on-chain — a fixed protocol share to pons and the rest to the creator-fee recipient.

Uniswap V3 — and V4 hooks ahead

Every coin's market is a Uniswap V3 WETH pool (1% fee tier); prices, quotes and swaps all run through the V3 stack. On the roadmap: once pons migrates, AURN will build on Uniswap V4 hooks for programmable pool logic and dynamic fees.

X-derived wallets

Signing in with X mints a wallet derived from your X user id via an HMAC of a server-held master secret. There's no stored key database; the key is re-derived on demand, and you can export it whenever you want.

10Security

  • Fixed supply & locked liquidity on every launch — no stealth mint, no rug of the pool.
  • Honeypot simulation. A buy-then-sell round-trip is simulated before you buy, so a token that can't be sold is caught up front.
  • Holder concentration is surfaced so you can see how distributed a coin is.
  • Quotes before signing. The amount you see is the amount that's encoded in the transaction.
Understand the model

Your X wallet is custodial: AURN can sign for it, which is what makes one-tap launching possible. Keep balances small, export your key for anything you want to fully self-custody, and always verify a coin's contract before trading.

12AI access (MCP)

AURN runs a Model Context Protocol server, so you can drive it from an AI client — Claude, Cursor, or anything that speaks MCP. Browse coins, get quotes, check your wallet, and (with your key) trade and launch, all by asking.

Endpoint

Add this as a remote MCP server in your client:

https://eska.fun/api/mcp

Get your key

Read tools work with no key. To trade or launch, generate a personal AURN API key in the app at Settings → AI access. Send it on every request as a bearer token:

Authorization: Bearer eska_mcp_…
Treat your key like a password

The key carries spend authority over your custodial wallet — anyone holding it can trade and launch as you. It's shown once, isn't stored, and should only ever go into an AI client you trust. Never paste it publicly.

Connect (Claude Desktop)

Add AURN under mcpServers in your client config, with the key in the auth header:

{ "mcpServers": { "eska": { "url": "https://eska.fun/api/mcp", "headers": { "Authorization": "Bearer eska_mcp_…" } } } }

Clients differ — some take a URL + header in their UI, others a JSON config like the above. Point any of them at the endpoint and set the bearer header.

13FAQ

Do I need a wallet or seed phrase?
No. Sign in with X and a wallet is minted for you. You can export its private key any time.
What chain is this?
Robinhood Chain — an Arbitrum Orbit L2, chain ID 4663, with ETH for gas.
How much does it cost to launch?
A small fixed fee (~0.0005 ETH) plus gas. An optional first buy can be included.
How do the fees work?
Every trade generates pool fees, split 60% creator / 30% protocol / 10% AURN. Claim your share anytime from the coin's page.
Is it safe?
Coins have fixed supply and locked liquidity, and a honeypot check runs before you buy. Your wallet is custodial, so keep balances modest and self-custody the rest.
What is $AURN?
The platform's deflationary token. Fees fund $AURN buyback & burn. $AURN is relaunching on @bankrbot; the new official contract will be announced at relaunch.
